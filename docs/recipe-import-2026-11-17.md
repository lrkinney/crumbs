## Review decisions

Excluded Quick & spicy chicken noodles because it requires leftover chicken; Spicy harissa bean stew because its yield explicitly includes leftovers; Mozzarella with spicy tomatoes because it requires an hour of marinating. Cauliflower uses the florets as well as slabs. Panang uses purchased curry paste instead of creating a surplus batch.

All recipes have full ingredient lists for the selected dinner, rewritten steps, tools, source URL and author, adaptation notes, timing estimates and portion guidance. Small-batch cooking still may leave raw package remainders; this is not a zero-grocery-waste guarantee.

Timing is editorial, not measured in a cooking trial. Prep time is not total hands-on time: frying and stirring are additional active work within cook time. No new recipe requires overnight preparation or cooked leftovers.

Nutrition is retained only for the asparagus/lemon spaghetti where the complete source quantities are retained. The other 26 adapted dinners need a full ingredient-based calculation. All 27 costs and images remain null; no unrelated food photo or zero-dollar cost has been assigned.

Food safety endpoints follow [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures). Recipes are source-reviewed and structurally validated, not kitchen-tested. Source content is not marked as licensed for public redistribution.

The five original prototype recipes are preserved, including their previously noted editorial gaps. This import does not certify those original recipes.
